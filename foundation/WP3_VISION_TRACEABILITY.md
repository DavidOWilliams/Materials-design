## Verification boundaries

- The numerical example records **mid_fraction=0.35** as mass-infeasible and **0.65** as constraint-satisfying and numerically qualified under its declared WP2 mesh. Both remain manufacturing-unknown in intended-only execution.
- An independent baseline retains the corrected thermal eigenstrain moment integral and mesh-doubling requirements. No new scientific constitutive law, predictor training or material calibration is introduced.
- The same campaign orchestration runs two materially different registered synthetic WP2-supported scenarios, with separate provider identities, dutiful brief/context hashes, and independent replay. Unregistered numerical evaluator identities cannot be silently substituted during recomputation. This demonstrates *software modularity only*, not a new physics model.
- `WP3_ACCEPTANCE_LEDGER.md` and `WP3_REVIEW_PACKAGE.md` are the acceptance and review records. **Gate 3 is not passed** absent independent scientific/architecture review. CI self-testing does not approve WP3, nor complete D1, WP4/5 or measured V1.

## WP3 architecture decision corrections R1–R4

The older schema-1 `wp3_campaign` is retired from accepted execution because its ledger integrity did not meet the verified schema-2 guarantee. Historical bytes remain accessible as explicitly unvalidated records. The `wp3_search` orchestration delegates starting design, construction and typed geometry checks to registered versioned scenario adapters instead of knowing an A/B homogeneous baseline or fixed 3-knot geometry. Its objective and constraint list and brief/catalogue identities are derived from the typed WP2 `Brief` and property pack. The alternate three-layer 380/320 K scenario runs through unchanged sequencing, budget, replay and Pareto mechanisms. `explore` remains structured seeded sampling. Recovery validates completed results even when other proposals are pending, and campaign termination is separately validated with a declared terminal event and bounded stop vocabulary. No change to the WP2 numerical scientific contracts or expansion to WP4/5 was made.
