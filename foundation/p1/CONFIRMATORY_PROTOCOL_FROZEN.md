# P1 scientific protocol — confirmatory v1, frozen before confirmatory outcomes

Protocol configuration: `foundation/p1/CONFIRMATORY_CONFIG_FROZEN.json`, committed at `4a4035459155d8f4af057477b667e6f168b26eb9` BEFORE any confirmatory execution.
Provisional document and pilot data are exploratory and are **not included in confirmatory results**.

## Primary question and hypotheses
Does the existing WP4 *density-guided areal-mass correction*, applied to subsequent WP3-generated candidates, improve numerically qualified feasible synthetic **estimated achieved-state** performance under matched proposal and numerical-work budgets compared with a blind seeded proposal sequence? H0: the stratified mean paired feasible-hypervolume effect is zero; H1: the effect is positive. Null/non-positive, negative and unresolved effects are acceptable. This is not a general optimiser study or physical validation.

## Frozen domain and groups
One WP3 neutral synthetic three-constituent catalogue, WP2 400/300 K 10-mm planar coupon, mass cap 31 kg/m² and existing WP4 ideal layer/feed transform; mesh 32 (exploratory qualification as explicitly returned by WP2). Strategies are sampling and structured exploratory coverage: both are proposal algorithms, **not** distinct inverse-optimisation algorithms. Paired feedback-off and feedback-on cases start from the same seed and WP3 proposal sequence; both use precisely four proposal slots, four WP4 transforms and up to four WP2 numerical evaluations. Failed attempts cost proposal slots. No shared cache; all cache hits zero by policy. The only search-state difference is feedback-on use of the previous arm's achieved areal-mass violation and the existing WP4 density-guided revision rule. Blind control does not execute the feedback calculation or use its own achieved scores to select subsequent proposals.

## Scenarios, trials and scope
Cartesian product: sampling/explore × feed-fraction-resolution 0.25/0.50. All use layer thickness 0.005 m, ideal planar route, mass cap 31, mesh 32. Twenty prespecified paired independent seeds 2001 through 2020 per scenario (80 paired comparisons; 160 arms). The four feasibility/runtime pilots (seeds 101–104) are excluded and may not be reused for confirmation. The feed/mesh values are supported *assumptions* and are not empirical manufacturing distortion magnitudes.

No general identity transform is available for heterogeneous architectures, and no independently calibrated holdout physical model exists. True identity and measured validation are **unsupported**, and should not be fabricated. A mass cap of 10 kg/m² may be used solely as a labelled no-feasible **challenge test**, never blended into the confirmatory sample. Methods C/D as a distinct algorithm are unsupported. The specified domain does not cover sintering, porosity, CFD, shrinkage or manufacturing residual stress.

## Frozen endpoint and budgets
Primary endpoint: across all 80 paired trials with equal weight to the four scenario strata, mean (feedback minus blind) normalized feasible three-objective hypervolume, at four proposal slots, obtained only from separately replayed WP4 achieved states and WP2 numerical evaluations.
Objectives minimize thermal_transmission (W/m²), areal_mass (kg/m²) and tensile_thermal_mismatch (Pa). The fixed, unmodified reference is (120,000 W/m²,45 kg/m²,1e9 Pa), chosen conservatively beyond the specified synthetic property extrema and mass cap, not fitted to confirmatory outcomes. Exact 3D union volume of feasible dominance boxes is normalized by the reference cuboid, with zero for no qualified feasible design. Missing/out-of-reference objectives are recorded and excluded from HV contribution but remain in trial denominator.

Secondary endpoints: per-scenario paired HV; trial-level probability of at least one qualified feasible candidate; objective vectors and tradeoffs; prefix HV at proposal budgets 1–4; matched-CPU prefix HV at **80,000,000 process CPU ns** on the same shared runner, with time budget fixed from exploratory pilot before confirmatory work; curves also show 20,40,60,80ms descriptive points. CPU timing is environment-conditional and includes generation, WP4 transform, WP2 evaluator and feedback calculation without double counting revision work. Stopping is at the last completed entire candidate whose cumulative CPU does not exceed the fixed threshold; no partial candidate scores. Independent final scoring CPU and evaluations are counted **separately** and excluded from both search budgets.

## Robustness evaluation, score separation and integrity
All frozen designs are replayed and rescored with exactly the same existing deterministic WP4+WP2 pipeline. This is a *common in-model scorer*, **not an independent manufacturing model**; it validates identical synthetic outcomes and evidence integrity only. As a prospective within-family parameter sensitivity check, each completed design is additionally scored under the other supported feed resolution (0.25 ↔ 0.50), not supplied to that run's search method. Search-path reconstruction always uses the original frozen process and verifies candidate/evaluation hashes before heldout rescoring. Separate scorer calls are outside optimisation budgets.

The raw pair records include complete candidate states, cost ledgers, failed attempts and hashes. A trial with a failed operation must retain an explicit failed outcome and consume its slot. Never drop failed trials from the denominator or impute numerical objectives. Aborted/incomplete comparisons remain unresolved and block a complete dataset claim; retain their logs. Unexpected unsupported values, state corruption, mismatched trials, absent objectives and unequal numerical work fail closed rather than silently exclude. Source SHA, dependencies, runner details, output hashes and manifest accompany evidence.

## Statistics, exclusion and interpretation
Paired nonparametric bootstrap percentile 95% intervals with 2000 paired resamples, fixed seed 8846. Bootstrap treats the 80 stratified pairs collectively for the primary mean with 20 pairs per stratum, and reports each of the four stratum estimates separately. No unadjusted scenario-level significance claims, no optional stopping, no favourable-case selection, no post-data protocol tuning. Secondary feasibility difference is expressed as paired trial-level percentage points. Prespecified engineering relevance screens: absolute normalized HV effect ≥0.02 and trial-level feasible-rate difference ≥10 percentage points. These are heuristic programme decision thresholds, not established aerospace design tolerances.

Evidence hierarchy: software and replay correctness; qualified WP2 numerical evaluation; synthetic estimated-state results; within-model assumption sensitivity; **no demonstrated generalisation or measured ceramic validation**.
